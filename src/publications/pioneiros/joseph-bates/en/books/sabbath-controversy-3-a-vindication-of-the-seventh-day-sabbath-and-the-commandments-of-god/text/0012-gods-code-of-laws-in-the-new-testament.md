### GOD’S CODE OF LAWS IN THE NEW TESTAMENT

“Why do ye transgress the commandments of God.” - Matthew 15:3

“What is written in the law, how readest thou?” - Luke 10:26

“Even as I have kept my Father’s commandments.” - John 15:10

“Yea, we establish the law.” - Romans 3:31 “The law is holy and the commandment is holy.” - Romans 7:12

“Not subject to the law of God.” - Romans 8:7, also 13:8-10

“But the commandments of God.” - 1st 1 Corinthians 7:19; 1st 1 Timothy 1:8

“For whoever shall keep the whole law,” etc. - James 2:10
